# CMB Simulator

An interactive browser-based simulation of cosmic microwave background
temperature anisotropy and polarization.

The simulator allows users to vary the cosmological density parameters for
normal matter, dark matter, and dark energy and explore their effects on the
CMB power spectrum and simulated sky.

The polarization mode generates E-mode polarization fields from precomputed
CAMB EE power spectra, with new statistical realizations generated in the
browser.

Originally based on the Planck CMB Simulator by Stuart Lowe and Chris North.

Extended and redesigned by Jonathan Byrd.
