Real telescopes do not observe the sky perfectly. Resolution, noise, beams, scanning strategy, and instrument response all affect what an experiment measures.

In this part of CMB School, we will explore how an imperfect instrument changes the sky we observe—and how scientists model those effects before drawing conclusions from the data.
